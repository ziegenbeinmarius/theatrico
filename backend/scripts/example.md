# Act 1

## Scene 1 - The Meeting

**ALICE:** Good morning, Robert. I wasn't expecting to see you here.

**ROBERT:** Nor I you. The world is a small stage, it seems.

**ALICE:** Indeed it is. How long has it been — five years?

**ROBERT:** Six. Since the night of the premiere.

**ALICE:** Ah. That night.

## Scene 2 - The Revelation

**ROBERT:** I never got a chance to explain myself.

**ALICE:** You had plenty of chances, Robert. You simply chose not to take them.

**ROBERT:** I was afraid.

**ALICE:** Of what?

**ROBERT:** Of the truth.

# Act 2

## Scene 1 - The Resolution

**ALICE:** Then tell me now. We have nothing left to lose.

**ROBERT:** Very well. I was the one who wrote the anonymous letter.

**ALICE:** I knew it was you.

**ROBERT:** Then why did you never say anything?

**ALICE:** Because I wanted to give you the chance to admit it yourself.

**ROBERT:** And if I never had?

**ALICE:** Then I would have waited until you did.
