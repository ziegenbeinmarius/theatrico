# Act 1

## Scene 1: The Kowalski Apartment

**BLANCHE:** They told me to take a streetcar named Desire, and then transfer to one called Cemeteries and ride six blocks and get off at—Elysian Fields!

**EUNICE:** That's where you are now.

**BLANCHE:** This—can this be—her home?

**STELLA:** Blanche!

**BLANCHE:** Stella, oh, Stella, Stella! Stella for Star!

**STELLA:** Now, Blanche!

**BLANCHE:** I thought you would never come back to this horrible place! What am I saying? I didn't mean to say that. I meant to be nice about it and say—Oh, what a convenient location and such—Ha-a-ha!

**STELLA:** Isn't it funny, I got in the habit of talking this way.

**BLANCHE:** I know, I know. But please don't expose me to it any more than you have to.

## Scene 2: Later That Evening

**STANLEY:** Who are you? One of those women?

**BLANCHE:** They call me Blanche DuBois. I'm Stella's sister.

**STANLEY:** Stella's sister, huh? You're not going to be the judge here.

**BLANCHE:** I understand there's to be a poker party tonight to which we ladies are cordially not invited.

**STANLEY:** Yeah? Where you from?

**BLANCHE:** Why, I'm from Laurel.

**STANLEY:** In Laurel, huh?

**BLANCHE:** Yes. That's right.

**STANLEY:** You were married once, weren't you?

**BLANCHE:** Yes. When I was quite young.

**STANLEY:** What happened?

**BLANCHE:** The boy—the boy died. I'm afraid I'm—going to be sick!

# Act 2

## Scene 1: The Apartment—A Few Weeks Later

**STANLEY:** You thought I didn't hear you two? I was listening. Right here. I heard every word you said.

**STELLA:** Please, Stanley.

**STANLEY:** You sit down! I've got something to tell you, and I'm gonna tell it while she's in there washing off the charm she put on him.

**STELLA:** This is making me so upset.

**STANLEY:** All right. I'll wait till she comes out of the bathroom.

**BLANCHE:** Hello, Stanley! Here I am, all freshly bathed and scented, and feeling like a brand new human being!

**STANLEY:** That's good.

**BLANCHE:** Is the coast clear?

**STANLEY:** She's been telling you things about me, hasn't she? About Stanley Kowalski?

**BLANCHE:** Why, Stanley!

**STANLEY:** I've been on to you from the start! Not once did you pull any wool over this boy's eyes!

**BLANCHE:** Now see here, Stanley. Let's start over, shall we, and just be friends.

## Scene 2: The Poker Night

**STELLA:** Stanley, you come out here and let Blanche finish dressing.

**STANLEY:** I'm waiting for Blanche to get out of the bathroom.

**BLANCHE:** Stanley, you've got to stop this. You've got to stop it.

**STANLEY:** Mitch is coming over. Mitch is very fond of Blanche.

**BLANCHE:** The music of the polka bands is wonderful! And they don't stop playing!

**MITCH:** Miss DuBois?

**BLANCHE:** Yes?

**MITCH:** May I have this dance?

**BLANCHE:** Why, I'd be delighted, Mr. Mitchell.

**MITCH:** My name's Harold. But they call me Mitch. You know how it is—sometimes I wonder if the other guys like me the way they say they do.

**BLANCHE:** Oh, they must adore you. I can tell. I can always recognize a kindred spirit.
