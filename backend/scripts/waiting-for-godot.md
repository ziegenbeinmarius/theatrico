# Act 1

## Scene 1: A Country Road

**ESTRAGON:** Nothing to be done.

**VLADIMIR:** I'm beginning to come round to that opinion. All my life I've tried to put it from me, saying, Vladimir, be reasonable, you haven't yet tried everything. And I resumed the struggle.

**ESTRAGON:** So there you are again.

**VLADIMIR:** Am I?

**ESTRAGON:** I'm glad to see you back. I thought you'd gone away for ever.

**VLADIMIR:** Me too.

**ESTRAGON:** Together again at last! We'll have to celebrate this. But how? Get up till I embrace you.

**VLADIMIR:** Not now, not now.

**ESTRAGON:** May one enquire where His Highness spent the night?

**VLADIMIR:** In a ditch.

**ESTRAGON:** A ditch! Where?

**VLADIMIR:** Over there.

**ESTRAGON:** And they didn't beat you?

**VLADIMIR:** Beat me? Certainly they beat me.

**ESTRAGON:** The same lot as usual?

**VLADIMIR:** The same? I don't know.

## Scene 2: Waiting

**ESTRAGON:** What do we do now?

**VLADIMIR:** Wait.

**ESTRAGON:** Yes, but while waiting.

**VLADIMIR:** What about hanging ourselves?

**ESTRAGON:** Hmm. It'd give us an erection.

**VLADIMIR:** An erection!

**ESTRAGON:** With all that follows. Where it falls mandrakes grow. That's why they shriek when you pull them up. Did you not know that?

**VLADIMIR:** Let's hang ourselves immediately!

**ESTRAGON:** From a bough? I wouldn't trust it.

**VLADIMIR:** We can always try.

**ESTRAGON:** Go ahead.

**VLADIMIR:** After you.

**ESTRAGON:** After you.

**VLADIMIR:** Let's wait for Godot.

**ESTRAGON:** Ah yes.

# Act 2

## Scene 1: The Next Day

**VLADIMIR:** Well? Shall we go?

**ESTRAGON:** Yes, let's go.

**VLADIMIR:** Well? Shall we go?

**ESTRAGON:** Yes, let's go.

**VLADIMIR:** Well, shall we?

**ESTRAGON:** I suppose so.

**VLADIMIR:** I don't know. They might come tomorrow.

**ESTRAGON:** And if he comes?

**VLADIMIR:** We'll be saved.

**ESTRAGON:** Well? Shall we go?

**VLADIMIR:** Pull on your trousers.

**ESTRAGON:** What?

**VLADIMIR:** Pull on your trousers.

**ESTRAGON:** You want me to pull off my trousers?

**VLADIMIR:** Pull ON your trousers.

**ESTRAGON:** True.

## Scene 2: Pozzo and Lucky

**POZZO:** I present to you Pozzo! Does that name mean nothing to you? I said does that name mean nothing to you?

**VLADIMIR:** We're not from these parts.

**POZZO:** On! Lucky, move!

**LUCKY:** Think, pig!

**POZZO:** His name is Lucky.

**ESTRAGON:** Funny that.

**POZZO:** Funny! It's a funny name for a slave. You may laugh.

**ESTRAGON:** We are not free to laugh at everything we want.

**POZZO:** True, true. On the way!

**LUCKY:** On!

**POZZO:** I cannot go without him. I might get lost. He knows the way. He'll show me the way.

**VLADIMIR:** It's the normal thing to do.

**ESTRAGON:** Is it?

**VLADIMIR:** Of course it is. The normal thing.

**POZZO:** On! I said on!
