<!-- title: Death of a Salesman -->

# Act 1

## Scene 1: The Kitchen

**WILLY:** I'm tired to the death.

**LINDA:** Willy, dear, talk to me.

**WILLY:** I couldn't make it. I just couldn't make it, Linda.

**LINDA:** But you're sixty years old. It's not a law that a man has to break his back at sixty.

**WILLY:** I'll start out in the morning. Maybe I'll feel better in the morning.

**LINDA:** Sleep well, darling.

**WILLY:** I'm the New England man. I'm vital in New England.

**LINDA:** There's no reason you can't work in New York.

**WILLY:** They don't need me in New York. I'm the New England man. I'm vital in New England.

## Scene 2: The Living Room

**BIFF:** He had a good dream. It's the only dream you can have.

**HAPPY:** To come out number-one man.

**BIFF:** That's right.

**HAPPY:** He fought it out here, and this is where I'm gonna win it for him.

**BIFF:** I know who I am, kid.

**HAPPY:** Well, take me along, will ya? I don't want to be flopping around like this.

**BIFF:** I'll get him a job. I'll get him a good job.

**HAPPY:** No, that's not what I—

**BIFF:** I'll see him next week. I'll call him and set a date.

# Act 2

## Scene 1: Howard's Office

**WILLY:** I named you. The minute you were born, I said to Howard—

**HOWARD:** That so?

**WILLY:** If it's a boy, I said, and it was a boy, I said, Howard Wagner.

**HOWARD:** I appreciate that, Willy, but there just is no spot here for you.

**WILLY:** But I gotta earn money, Howard. I'm in no position to—

**HOWARD:** Where are your sons? Why don't your sons give you a hand?

**WILLY:** They're working on a very big deal.

**HOWARD:** This is no time for false pride, Willy. You go to your sons and tell them that you're tired.

**WILLY:** Oh, no question, no question.

**HOWARD:** Then that's that, heh?

**WILLY:** All right, I'll go to Boston tomorrow.

**HOWARD:** No, no.

**WILLY:** I can't throw myself on my sons. I'm not a cripple!

**HOWARD:** Look, kid, I'm busy this morning.

## Scene 2: The Restaurant

**BIFF:** Why am I trying to become what I don't want to be? What am I doing in an office, making a contemptuous, begging fool of myself, when all I want is out there, waiting for me the minute I say I know who I am!

**HAPPY:** Sure, sure.

**BIFF:** Why can't I say that to him? Why can't I say that, Hap?

**HAPPY:** You're not that guy. You're the man.

**BIFF:** I am not a leader of men, Willy, and neither are you. You were never anything but a hard-working drummer who landed in the ash can like all the rest of them!

**WILLY:** I am not a dime a dozen! I am Willy Loman, and you are Biff Loman!

**BIFF:** Pop, I'm nothing! I'm nothing, Pop. Can't you understand that? There's no spite in it any more. I'm just what I am, that's all.

**WILLY:** What're you doing? What're you doing? Why is he crying?

**LINDA:** He loves you, Willy.

**HAPPY:** He loves you, Pop.

**WILLY:** Always loved me. Isn't that a remarkable thing? Ben, he'll worship me for it.
